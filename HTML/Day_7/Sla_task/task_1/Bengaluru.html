Welcome Our Bengaluru Company

As mentioned, Bangalore is home to approximately 5,000 to over 10,000 active IT companies, depending on how strictly you define them.If you count every registered tech startup, micro-enterprise, and digital agency alongside major corporations, some estimates exceed 25,000 tech entities across the Greater Bengaluru region.

🚀 Tech Ecosystem Breakdown

  • Global MNCs: Over 1,000 multinational tech giants (like Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and Cisco) have established global capability centres (GCCs) or major campuses here.
  • Tech Workforce: The city employs over 1.5 million IT professionals, accounting for nearly 35% to 40% of India's total tech exports.
  • Startup Capital: Bangalore is home to more than 15,000 active startups, including the highest concentration of tech unicorns in India (like Flipkart, Swiggy, and Razorpay).

Product based companies

Bengaluru contains over 3,500 established software product-based companies and active deep-tech startups. While tracking platforms like AmbitionBox list roughly 1,500+ major software product operations, the broader ecosystem features three core categories of product builders.

Categorized Breakdown of Product Companies

Global MNCs & Tech Giants: Over 300 global tech giants have massive R&D hubs or primary global capability centres (GCCs) in Bengaluru. Key entities include Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Oracle, Cisco, and Intel.

B2C Consumer Tech Unicorns: The city serves as the corporate headquarters for 300 global tech giants that build internal software stacks, including Flipkart, Swiggy, PhonePe, Razorpay, CRED, Ola, and Groww.

B2B SaaS & Enterprise Software Builders: Roughly 1,200 to 1,500 registered companies build business-facing software products. Prominent players with significant regional headquarters or engineering workforces include Postman, Freshworks, Zoho, Atlassian, and Tally Solutions.

Top comapies

  • Google: Internet services, cloud infrastructure, and AI development. Located at RMZ Infinity (Old Madras Road) and Bagmane Constellation Business Park. Typical SDE-2 salary is ₹45 LPA.
  • Microsoft : Cloud computing (Azure), enterprise tools, and core OS development. Located in Bellandur (Outer Ring Road) and Prestige Ferns Galaxy. Typical SDE-2 salary is ₹42–46 LPA.

Service based IT companies

Bengaluru is home to over 4,000 service-based IT companies, ranging from massive domestic conglomerates to specialized global consulting firms. These companies primarily provide digital transformation, software maintenance, cloud migration, and outsourced engineering services to worldwide clients.

Top companies

  • Flipkart: E-commerce, supply chain algorithms, and retail AI solutions. Headquartered in Cessna Business Park (Kadubeesanahalli). Typical SDE-2 salary is ₹28 LPA.
  • Amazon India: E-commerce architecture, logistics automation, and AWS infrastructure. Headquartered at the World Trade Center (Malleswaram). Typical SDE-2 salary is ₹36 LPA.
