Welcom our IT companies

Hyderabad is home to over 1,500 established IT and IT-enabled services (ITES) companies. This ecosystem includes more than 1,200 registered software firms, ranging from global tech giants like Microsoft and Google to hundreds of startups and massive Global Capability Centers (GCCs).

Why This Shift Matters For You

The Talent Premium: Companies are heavily prioritizing specialized skills over general engineering. Growth is concentrated in Cloud Architecture, GenAI/Machine Learning, Data Engineering, and Cybersecurity.

The Infrastructure Pivot: Investment is moving rapidly toward the west, specifically Kokapet Neopolis, which is being built explicitly to handle massive data, AI workloads, and global corporate headquarters.

Global Ownership: Working in a modern Hyderabad GCC means you are no longer just an outsourced vendor; you are building core proprietary platforms directly for the global enterprise.

Product based companies

There are more than 1,130 product-based IT and software companies actively operating in Hyderabad.

Breakdown of the 1,130+ Product Entities

Global Tech Anchors: Massive software development centres for elite firms like Microsoft India (its largest software hub outside the US), Google, Amazon, Apple, Meta, and Uber.

Enterprise Software Multinationals: Major B2B software, infrastructure, and automation platforms including Oracle, Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, Amdocs, Adobe, and Blue Yonder.

Indian SaaS & AI Scale-ups: Fast-growing domestic players that build proprietary tech products locally, such as Darwinbox, HighRadius, Zenoti, and SmartDocs Inc.

Top companies

Microsoft India: Operating out of Gachibowli, its Hyderabad Development Center (IDC) stands as Microsoft's largest software development hub outside the United States. SDE-2 roles average around ₹40 LPA.

Amazon Web Services: Boasting its largest corporate office building globally in Hyderabad's Financial District, Amazon’s engineering teams work on core AWS cloud architecture, digital streaming infrastructure, and global e-commerce systems. SDE-2 packages routinely range from ₹30.5 LPA to ₹33.7 LPA

There are more than 5,000 IT services and consulting companies operating in Hyderabad

The Scale of Service Companies

The Mass Tech Employers (Tier 1): Mega-corporations handling end-to-end global digital transformation, infrastructure operations, and legacy systems maintenance. This includes Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) (which operates multiple massive campuses in Adibatla and Gachibowli), Infosys (Pocharam & Gachibowli), Wipro, Cognizant, and Tech Mahindra

Global Consulting Agencies: High-end strategic technology services, cloud orchestration, and corporate operational structuring. Elite firms like Accenture, Capgemini, Deloitte, HCLTech, Genpact, and LTM Limited drive immense project pipelines out of spaces like Salarpuria Sattva Knowledge City and Mindspace.

Mid-Sized & Boutique Agencies: Thousands of smaller, agile software agencies housing 20 to 500 engineers, focusing on target skill fields like specialized Salesforce cloud consulting, custom mobile applications, digital marketing tools, and offshore corporate IT training.

Top companies

Tech Mahindra: Runs key delivery centers in HITEC City, Madhapur, and Bahadurpally focused on telecom, network architecture, and customer support.

Deloitte: Anchored in HITEC City, their Hyderabad wing functions as a massive technological arm driving global enterprise risk and digital cloud consultancy.
