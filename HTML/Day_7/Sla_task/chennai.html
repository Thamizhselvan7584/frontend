Chennai is India’s second-largest IT and software exporter and a major hub for global SaaS and enterprise tech. The city's top IT employers and software firms span across major technology parks like SIPCOT (Siruseri), DLF (Ramapuram), and Ascendas (Taramani).

Total comapines in chennai: 5,000

The total registered count of active IT and ITES (Information Technology Enabled Services) companies in Chennai is over 5,000 corporate entities.

Structural Breakdown of Tech Entities

Active Operational Tech Entities: Over 2,000 companies function as active, independent tech employers, product creators, and localized software services providers.

Global Multinational Delivery Centres: More than 100 prominent global MNCs—such as TCS, Cognizant, Accenture, and IBM—operate massive, multi-campus offshore engineering facilities.

Registered Sub-branches & ITES Agencies: Including all local operational branches, individual business process management units (BPMs), and digital outsourcing agencies, the wider figure reaches upwards of 4,000 entities.

Product-based companies

Chennai is home to over 700 software product-based companies. This includes home-grown global software-as-a-service (SaaS) unicorns, specialized fintech platforms, and deep-tech startups. Additionally, the city hosts major engineering and research & development (R&D) hubs for over 50 international tech giants

  • Freshworks: A globally traded SaaS platform providing top-tier customer relationship management (CRM) and IT service tools.

Service Based Company
