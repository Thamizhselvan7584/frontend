The Entaier Story of Drogon Ball:

Long ago, in a world filled with strange creatures and powerful fighters, a cheerful boy named Goku set out on an adventure to find seven magical orbs called Dragon Balls. When brought together, these glowing spheres could summon a mighty dragon who would grant any wish. Along the way, Goku met brave friends like Bulma, Krillin, and Master Roshi, and faced strong rivals who tested his courage and strength. Through battles, laughter, and challenges, Goku grew not just in power, but in heart, learning that the greatest treasures were friendship, kindness, and the will to never give up.
