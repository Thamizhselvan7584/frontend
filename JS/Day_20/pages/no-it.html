DEPENDENCY MAP

Why One Failure Can Become a Chain Reaction

💳 Payments → Business

When digital payment systems become unavailable, stores may need cash or offline procedures. Delayed payments can affect customers, suppliers and employees.

📡 Communication → Emergency

Network disruption can make messaging, cloud calling and online coordination harder. Emergency teams need alternate communication channels and clear escalation plans.

🏥 Digital Records → Care

Healthcare services may depend on electronic records, scheduling and connected devices. Downtime procedures should let staff safely access essential information and continue care.

🚆 Transport → Supply Chains

Ticketing, navigation, logistics and traffic systems can depend on digital services. Manual dispatch and local operating procedures can reduce the impact of outages.

🏦 Banking → Confidence

Financial services need availability as well as security. During outages, transparent communication, transaction reconciliation and recovery procedures help protect trust.

🏛️ Government → Public Services

Digital government portals can simplify access to services, but citizens still need alternatives during downtime. Resilient design keeps critical services accessible.

RESILIENCE CHECKLIST

How an Organization Can Prepare

Before an outage

Identify critical services, map dependencies, maintain tested backups, document manual procedures, train staff and define communication plans.

During an outage

Protect people first, confirm the scope, switch to safe fallback procedures, prioritize critical services and communicate verified information.

After recovery

Validate restored systems, reconcile transactions and data, monitor for hidden failures and document what happened.

Improve continuously

Turn lessons into updated procedures, architecture changes, training and regular recovery exercises. Resilience is an ongoing capability.
