PREDICTION GUIDE

What makes a strong prediction?

Be specific

Say what will change, who will experience it and the approximate time horizon.

Show your reasoning

Connect your idea to a visible trend, technology capability, social need or known challenge.

Accept uncertainty

Confidence is not certainty. A useful forecast explains how strongly you believe it may happen.
