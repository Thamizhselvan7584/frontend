Engineering College Departments

CSE

Computer Science and Engineering focuses on programming, software development, AI, and data science.

IT

Information Technology deals with networking, databases, web development, and cloud computing.

ECE

Electronics and Communication Engineering focuses on communication systems and embedded technology.

EEE

Electrical and Electronics Engineering studies electrical machines, power systems, and automation.

Mechanical

Mechanical Engineering focuses on machine design, manufacturing, robotics, and thermal engineering.
